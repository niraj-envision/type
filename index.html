    settings

    sound on click

    a synthesised switch, generated in the browser — no audio files.

    volume

    how loud the keys are.

    space bar

    the sound at the end of a word: the same click as a letter, a deeper one, or nothing.

    error sound

    a short buzz when you hit the wrong key.

    word list

    what the generated modes draw from: the english 400, the english 1k, or code.

    theme

    colours for the whole page.

    caret

    the shape that follows your cursor.

    smooth caret

    animate the caret between letters.

    pace caret

    a second, dimmer caret that moves at a steady speed — your personal best for this test, your last run, or a number you choose.

    stop on error

    refuse the keypress, or stop at the end of a wrong word.

    blind mode

    no feedback while typing. accuracy is still measured.

    live wpm

    show speed while you type.

    layout

    a wrapped paragraph, or one straight line with the word you are typing held in the middle.

    font

    the typeface of the test text.

    font weight

    heavier stems read sharper as light text on a dark ground.

    font size

    size of the test text.

    leaderboard

    leaderboard name

    the name shown next to your runs. nothing else about you is sent.

    your data

    results, personal bests and per-key history live only in this browser. take them with you as a file, or bring a file back.

    reset everything

    clears your results, personal bests and per-key history.
